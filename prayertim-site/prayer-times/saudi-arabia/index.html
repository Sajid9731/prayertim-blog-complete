    About prayer times in Saudi Arabia

    Saudi Arabia is listed here with 25 major cities, starting with Riyadh. All cities use the Asia/Riyadh time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The Umm al-Qura method sets fajr at 18.5°, isha 90 minutes after maghrib. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Saudi Arabia today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 25 cities in Saudi Arabia. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Saudi Arabia?

    This site defaults to the Umm al-Qura University, Makkah method (Fajr at 18.5°, Isha 90 minutes after Maghrib) for Saudi Arabia, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Saudi Arabia?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Saudi Arabia can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
