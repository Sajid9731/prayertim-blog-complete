    About prayer times in Turkey

    Turkey is listed here with 41 major cities, starting with Ankara. All cities use the Europe/Istanbul time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The Turkey method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Turkey today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 41 cities in Turkey. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Turkey?

    This site defaults to the Turkey (Diyanet style) method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for Turkey, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Turkey?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Turkey can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
