    About prayer times in Spain

    Spain is listed here with 14 major cities, starting with Madrid. The country covers 3 time zones, so every city page uses its own local time. Some or all of the country changes its clocks seasonally; the timetables follow the local clock automatically.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Spain today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 14 cities in Spain. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Spain?

    This site defaults to the Muslim World League method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for Spain, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Spain?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Spain can differ by several minutes even on the same day, and the country spans more than one time zone.
