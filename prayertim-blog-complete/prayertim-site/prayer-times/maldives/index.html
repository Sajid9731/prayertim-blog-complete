    About prayer times in Maldives

    Maldives is listed here with 3 major cities, starting with Male. All cities use the Indian/Maldives time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The Karachi method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 18°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    What are the prayer times in Maldives today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 3 cities in Maldives. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Maldives?

    This site defaults to the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 18°) for Maldives, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Maldives?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Maldives can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
