    About prayer times in United States

    United States is listed here with 52 major cities, starting with Washington, D.C.. The country covers 10 time zones, so every city page uses its own local time. Some or all of the country changes its clocks seasonally; the timetables follow the local clock automatically.

    The ISNA method sets fajr at 15°, isha at 15°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in United States today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 52 cities in United States. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in United States?

    This site defaults to the Islamic Society of North America (ISNA) method (Fajr at 15°, Isha at 15°) for United States, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in United States?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in United States can differ by several minutes even on the same day, and the country spans more than one time zone.
