    About prayer times in South Korea

    South Korea is listed here with 8 major cities, starting with Seoul. All cities use the Asia/Seoul time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in South Korea today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 8 cities in South Korea. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in South Korea?

    This site defaults to the Muslim World League method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for South Korea, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in South Korea?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in South Korea can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
