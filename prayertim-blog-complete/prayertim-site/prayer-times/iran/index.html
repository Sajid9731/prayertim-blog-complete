    About prayer times in Iran

    Iran is listed here with 18 major cities, starting with Tehran. All cities use the Asia/Tehran time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The Tehran method sets fajr at 17.7°, isha at 14°, maghrib at 4.5° below the horizon. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Iran today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 18 cities in Iran. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Iran?

    This site defaults to the Institute of Geophysics, University of Tehran method (Fajr at 17.7°, Isha at 14°, Maghrib at 4.5° below the horizon) for Iran, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Iran?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Iran can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
