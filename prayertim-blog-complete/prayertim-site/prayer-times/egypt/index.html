    About prayer times in Egypt

    Egypt is listed here with 25 major cities, starting with Cairo. All cities use the Africa/Cairo time zone. Some or all of the country changes its clocks seasonally; the timetables follow the local clock automatically.

    The Egyptian method sets fajr at 19.5°, isha at 17.5°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Egypt today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 25 cities in Egypt. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Egypt?

    This site defaults to the Egyptian General Authority of Survey method (Fajr at 19.5°, Isha at 17.5°) for Egypt, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Egypt?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Egypt can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
