    About prayer times in Thailand

    Thailand is listed here with 12 major cities, starting with Bangkok. All cities use the Asia/Bangkok time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Thailand today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 12 cities in Thailand. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Thailand?

    This site defaults to the Muslim World League method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for Thailand, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Thailand?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Thailand can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
