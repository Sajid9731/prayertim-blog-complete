    India is set to the Karachi method by default on this site because it is widely used in the region.     Frequently asked questions about prayer times in Gwalior

    What are today's prayer times in Gwalior?

    They are shown at the top of this page. They are calculated for Gwalior's coordinates and its time zone (Asia/Kolkata), and the page refreshes them automatically every day, so you always see the current date.

    Which calculation method is used for Gwalior?

    By default this page uses the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 18°), which is the usual choice for India. You can change the method in the Options box above if your local authority or mosque uses another one.

    How is Asr time calculated in Gwalior?

    The default here is the Hanafi calculation, where Asr begins when an object's shadow is twice its length (plus the noon shadow), because most local Muslims follow the Hanafi school. Use the Options box to switch to Standard Asr, which begins later.

    Does Gwalior change its clocks for daylight saving time?

    Gwalior does not change its clocks during the year (UTC+5:30), so prayer times move only with the sun.

    What is the Qibla direction in Gwalior?

    The Qibla from Gwalior is about 271° clockwise from true north (W), roughly 3,924 km along the great circle to the Kaaba in Makkah. A compass shows magnetic north, which differs slightly from true north, so check your local magnetic declination.

    Can I use these times instead of my local mosque timetable?

    These times are calculated from astronomy and are a reliable guide, but many mosques publish their own adhan and iqamah times, sometimes with fixed adjustments. If your mosque publishes a timetable, follow it for congregational prayers.
