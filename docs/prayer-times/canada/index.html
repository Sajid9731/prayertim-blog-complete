    About prayer times in Canada

    Canada is listed here with 15 major cities, starting with Ottawa. The country covers 7 time zones, so every city page uses its own local time. Some or all of the country changes its clocks seasonally; the timetables follow the local clock automatically.

    The ISNA method sets fajr at 15°, isha at 15°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Canada today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 15 cities in Canada. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Canada?

    This site defaults to the Islamic Society of North America (ISNA) method (Fajr at 15°, Isha at 15°) for Canada, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Canada?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Canada can differ by several minutes even on the same day, and the country spans more than one time zone.
