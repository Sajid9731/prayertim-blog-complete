    About prayer times in Palau

    Palau is listed here with 2 major cities, starting with Ngerulmud. All cities use the Pacific/Palau time zone. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The ISNA method sets fajr at 15°, isha at 15°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Which calculation method is used in Palau?

    This site defaults to the Islamic Society of North America (ISNA) method (Fajr at 15°, Isha at 15°) for Palau, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Palau?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Palau can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
