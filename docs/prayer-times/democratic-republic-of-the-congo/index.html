    About prayer times in Democratic Republic of the Congo

    Democratic Republic of the Congo is listed here with 5 major cities, starting with Kinshasa. The country covers 2 time zones, so every city page uses its own local time. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Which calculation method is used in Democratic Republic of the Congo?

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Democratic Republic of the Congo?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Democratic Republic of the Congo can differ by several minutes even on the same day, and the country spans more than one time zone.
