    About prayer times in Brazil

    Brazil is listed here with 11 major cities, starting with Brasilia. The country covers 5 time zones, so every city page uses its own local time. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Brazil today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 11 cities in Brazil. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Brazil?

    This site defaults to the Muslim World League method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for Brazil, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Brazil?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Brazil can differ by several minutes even on the same day, and the country spans more than one time zone.
