    About prayer times in Norway

    Norway is listed here with 4 major cities, starting with Oslo. All cities use the Europe/Oslo time zone. Some or all of the country changes its clocks seasonally; the timetables follow the local clock automatically.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Standard Asr is the default; Asr begins when a shadow equals the object's length. Hanafi followers can switch to Hanafi Asr, which begins later. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Norway today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 4 cities in Norway. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Norway?

    This site defaults to the Muslim World League method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for Norway, with Standard Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Norway?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Norway can differ by several minutes even on the same day.
