    About prayer times in Kazakhstan

    Kazakhstan is listed here with 11 major cities, starting with Astana. The country covers 4 time zones, so every city page uses its own local time. Clocks do not change seasonally, so prayer times shift only with the sun through the year.

    The MWL method sets fajr at 18°, isha at 17°. Hanafi Asr is the default because most Muslims in this region follow the Hanafi school; Asr begins when a shadow is twice the object's length. For the reasoning behind these choices see calculation methods compared and our methodology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the prayer times in Kazakhstan today?

    The table at the top of this page lists today's Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for 11 cities in Kazakhstan. Select a city to see its full monthly timetable.

    Which calculation method is used in Kazakhstan?

    This site defaults to the Muslim World League method (Fajr at 18°, Isha at 17°) for Kazakhstan, with Hanafi Asr. You can change both in the Options box.

    Do prayer times differ between cities in Kazakhstan?

    Yes. Prayer times depend on a city's latitude and longitude, so cities in Kazakhstan can differ by several minutes even on the same day, and the country spans more than one time zone.
