    Accurate namaz times, explained

    prayertim.com calculates the five daily prayer times from the position of the sun at your exact location. Fajr begins at true dawn, Dhuhr just after the sun passes its highest point, Asr when an object's shadow reaches a set length, Maghrib at sunset and Isha when evening twilight ends. Because every one of these depends on latitude, longitude and the date, a timetable for one city cannot be copied to another a few hundred kilometres away.

    Scholars and institutions use slightly different angles for Fajr and Isha, and two schools of thought define Asr differently. We start each country on the method most commonly used there and let you change it in one click. Read the full explanation on our methodology page or in our guides below.
